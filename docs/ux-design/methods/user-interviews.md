# User Interviews

**Type:** Qualitative Research
**When:** Discovery phase, before or after usability testing
**Difficulty:** Intermediate

## What Are User Interviews?

One-to-one structured or semi-structured conversations with users or potential users. The goal is to understand their context, goals, mental models, behaviours, and pain points — not to test a specific design.

> Interviews tell you *why*. Analytics tell you *what*. Both are necessary.

## When to Use

- Early discovery — understanding the problem space before designing anything
- After usability testing — to understand *why* users behaved a certain way
- Evaluating existing products — uncovering frustrations and workarounds

## Planning an Interview

### 1. Define your research questions
What do you *need to know* (not just want to know)?

> Example: "What are the biggest friction points in a freelancer's invoicing process?"

### 2. Recruit participants
- **5–8 participants** is sufficient for qualitative insight
- Match participants to your target user group
- Avoid recruiting friends or colleagues (biased responses)

### 3. Write a discussion guide (not a script)
- Open with warm-up questions (background, daily routine)
- Move to your core topic areas
- Close with open-ended reflection

## Conducting the Interview

**Do:**
- Use open-ended questions: "Tell me about…", "Walk me through…", "How do you currently…"
- Follow up with "Why?" and "Can you tell me more?"
- Stay silent when users pause — they often continue with the most useful insights
- Record (with permission) so you can focus on listening, not note-taking

**Don't:**
- Ask leading questions: "Do you find this confusing?" → ask "What is your reaction to this?"
- Mention your product or solution
- React visibly to answers (positive or negative)
- Ask what users *want* — ask what they *do*

## Analysis

1. Transcribe or timestamp recordings
2. **Affinity mapping** — write insights on sticky notes; group by theme
3. Identify patterns across participants
4. Synthesise into **research insights**, not raw quotes

## Output

- Key insight statements: *"Users consistently described X as…"*
- Opportunity areas for design
- Input for [Personas](../glossary/p) and [Empathy Maps](./empathy-mapping)
