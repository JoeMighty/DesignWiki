# U

## Usability
**Category:** UX Fundamentals · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The ease with which users can achieve their goals effectively, efficiently, and satisfactorily. **Jakob Nielsen** defines usability across five dimensions:

1. **Learnability** — how easy is it for new users?
2. **Efficiency** — how quickly can experienced users perform tasks?
3. **Memorability** — can users return after a gap and remember how?
4. **Errors** — how often do users make errors, and how severe?
5. **Satisfaction** — is the experience pleasant?

**Related:** [Heuristic Evaluation](./h), [Usability Testing](./u), [Cognitive Load](./c)

## Usability Testing
**Category:** Research Methods · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

A research method in which real users attempt to complete tasks with a product while an observer watches and records what happens. The goal is to identify where users struggle — not to prove the design works.

> 5 users will reveal ~85% of usability problems (Nielsen's Law).

### Moderated vs Unmoderated

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Moderated | Researcher present, can probe and follow up |
| Unmoderated | Remote, automated — scales easily |

**Related:** [Heuristic Evaluation](./h), [Prototype](./p), [Think-Aloud Protocol](./t)

## User Journey Map
**Category:** Research Tools · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

A visualisation of the end-to-end experience a user has with a product or service — across time, touchpoints, channels, and emotional state. Reveals gaps, friction points, and opportunities missed by looking at screens alone.

**Related:** [Persona](./p), [Empathy Map](./e), [Service Design](./s)
