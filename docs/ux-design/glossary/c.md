# C

## Card Sorting
**Category:** Research Methods · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

A UX research method in which participants organise topics written on cards into groups that make sense to them. Used to understand users' mental models and inform information architecture decisions.

> **Open card sort:** participants create and name their own groups.
> **Closed card sort:** participants sort cards into pre-defined categories.

**Related:** [Information Architecture](./i), [Mental Model](./m), [Tree Testing](./t)

## CTA (Call to Action)
**Category:** UI Design · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A design element — typically a button or link — that prompts the user to take a specific action: "Sign Up", "Buy Now", "Learn More". Effective CTAs are clear, prominent, and benefit-focused.

**Related:** [Affordance](./a), [Hierarchy](../graphic-design/glossary/h), [Conversion](./c)

---

## Cognitive Load
**Category:** Usability · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

The total mental effort required to use an interface. Excessive cognitive load causes errors and abandonment. UX design aims to minimise unnecessary cognitive load through clarity, chunking, and progressive disclosure.

> **Types:** Intrinsic (task complexity), Extraneous (poor design), Germane (learning).

**Related:** [Progressive Disclosure](./p), [Mental Model](./m), [Usability](./u)
