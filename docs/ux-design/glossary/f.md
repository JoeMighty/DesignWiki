# F

## Fitts's Law
**Category:** Interaction Design · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

A predictive model of human movement: **the time to reach a target is a function of the distance to and size of the target**. Larger, closer targets are faster and easier to click or tap.

> Practical implications: make buttons big enough, keep primary actions close to where the user already is (e.g. bottom-of-screen on mobile), and avoid tiny tap targets.

**Related:** [Affordance](./a), [Motor Accessibility](./m), [Touch Target](./t)

## Fidelity
**Category:** Design Process · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The level of detail and interactivity in a prototype or design artefact.

| Fidelity | Description | When to use |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| Low | Sketches, paper prototypes | Early ideation, cheap iteration |
| Mid | Wireframes, greyscale mockups | Structure and flow validation |
| High | Pixel-perfect, interactive prototypes | Usability testing, stakeholder sign-off |

**Related:** [Wireframe](./w), [Prototype](./p), [Mockup](../graphic-design/glossary/m)

---

## Flow (User Flow)
**Category:** Information Architecture · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A diagram mapping the steps a user takes to complete a task within a product. User flows identify decision points, potential friction, and opportunities to streamline the experience.

**Related:** [User Journey](./u), [Information Architecture](./i), [Task Analysis](./t)
