# V

## Value
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The lightness or darkness of a colour, independent of its hue. High-value colours are light (close to white); low-value colours are dark (close to black). Value contrast is the most important factor in making a design readable.

**Related:** [Hue](./h), [Saturation](./s), [Tint](./t), [Shade](./s)

---

## Vector
**Category:** Digital · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A mathematical, resolution-independent image format built from points, paths, and curves (e.g. SVG, AI, EPS). Vectors can be scaled to any size without quality loss, making them ideal for logos and illustrations.

**Related:** [Raster](./r), [Resolution](./r), [Path](./p)
