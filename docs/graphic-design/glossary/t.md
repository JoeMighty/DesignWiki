# T

## Tracking
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The uniform adjustment of spacing between all characters in a block of text. Increasing tracking (positive) opens up text for a lighter, more open feel; decreasing it (negative) tightens text for a compact, dense look.

> Distinct from **kerning**, which adjusts specific character pairs.

**Related:** [Kerning](./k), [Leading](./l), [Ligature](./l)

---

## Trim
**Category:** Print · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The final edge of a printed piece after it has been cut to size. Artwork should extend to the bleed line (typically 3 mm beyond the trim) to avoid white edges.

**Related:** [Bleed](./b), [Crop Marks](./c), [Safe Zone](./s)

---

## Typeface
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A family of fonts that share a unified design — e.g. **Helvetica**. The typeface is the design; a **font** is a specific weight, style, or size within that family (e.g. Helvetica Bold 12pt).

**Related:** [Font](./f), [Serif](./s), [Weight](./w)

---

## Triadic
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

A colour scheme using three colours evenly spaced around the colour wheel (120° apart), such as red, yellow, and blue. Triadic schemes offer strong contrast while maintaining balance.

**Related:** [Complementary Colours](./c), [Analogous Colours](./a), [Hue](./h)
