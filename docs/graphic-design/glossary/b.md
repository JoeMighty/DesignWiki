# B

## Balance
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The visual distribution of weight in a composition. Can be **symmetrical** (mirrored), **asymmetrical** (different but equally weighted elements), or **radial** (elements arranged around a central point).

**Related:** [Hierarchy](./h), [White Space](./w), [Alignment](./a)

---

## Baseline
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The invisible horizontal line on which most letters sit. Descenders (like **g** and **p**) extend below the baseline. Baseline grids help align type across a multi-column layout.

**Related:** [Ascender](./a), [Descender](./d), [Leading](./l)

---

## Bleed
**Category:** Print · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

Artwork that extends beyond the trim edge of a printed page — typically 3 mm. Bleed ensures there are no white borders if the cut is slightly off.

**Related:** [Trim](./t), [Crop Marks](./c), [Safe Zone](./s)

---

## Body Text
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The main block of readable copy in a design, as opposed to headings or captions. Body text is typically set between 9–12 pt in print and 14–18 px on screen.

**Related:** [Leading](./l), [Tracking](./t), [Legibility](./l)
