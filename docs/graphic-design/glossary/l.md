# L

## Leading
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The vertical space between lines of type, measured from baseline to baseline. The term originates from the strips of lead used to separate lines in metal typesetting. Too little leading makes text feel cramped; too much can break visual flow.

**Related:** [Baseline](./b), [Kerning](./k), [Tracking](./t)

---

## Legibility
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

How easily individual characters in a typeface can be distinguished from one another. Legibility is a property of the typeface itself — influenced by x-height, stroke contrast, and letterform clarity.

> Contrast with **readability**, which refers to ease of reading extended text.

**Related:** [Readability](./r), [X-height](./x), [Contrast](./c)

---

## Ligature
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

A single glyph that combines two or more characters to avoid awkward spacing or collisions — the most common being **fi**, **fl**, **ff**, **ffi**, and **ffl**.

**Related:** [Glyph](./g), [Kerning](./k), [Typeface](./t)
