# W

## White Space
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

Also called **negative space** — the empty area between and around design elements. White space is not wasted space; it gives content room to breathe, improves legibility, and communicates sophistication.

**Related:** [Margin](./m), [Proximity](./p), [Hierarchy](./h)

---

## Wireframe
**Category:** Digital · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A low-fidelity skeletal layout showing the structure and hierarchy of a screen or page without visual design applied. Wireframes focus on layout, content placement, and user flow, not aesthetics.

**Related:** [Mockup](./m), [Prototype](./p)
