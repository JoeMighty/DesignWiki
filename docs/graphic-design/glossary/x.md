# X

## X-height
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The height of a typeface's lowercase letters, measured from the baseline to the top of the letter **x**. A large x-height improves legibility at small sizes, which is why many screen typefaces have relatively tall x-heights.
