# A

## Alignment
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The positioning of visual elements along a common edge or axis — left, right, centred, or justified. Consistent alignment creates order and makes layouts easier to read.

**Related:** [Grid](./g), [White Space](./w), [Hierarchy](./h)

---

## Analogous Colours
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

Colours that sit adjacent to one another on the colour wheel (e.g. blue, blue-green, green). Analogous palettes feel harmonious and are often found in nature.

**Related:** [Complementary Colours](./c), [Triadic](./t), [Hue](./h)

---

## Ascender
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The part of a lowercase letter that rises above the x-height — seen in letters such as **b**, **d**, **f**, **h**, **k**, **l**, and **t**.

**Related:** [Descender](./d), [X-height](./x), [Baseline](./b)

---

## Aspect Ratio
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The proportional relationship between the width and height of an image or design element (e.g. 16:9, 4:3, 1:1). Maintaining aspect ratio prevents distortion when resizing.
