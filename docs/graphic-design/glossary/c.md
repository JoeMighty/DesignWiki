# C

## CMYK
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key (Black)** — the four ink colours used in standard four-colour offset printing. CMYK is a subtractive colour model: mixing all colours produces black (in theory); in practice, a separate black ink is used for depth and economy.

**Related:** [RGB](./r), [Spot Colour](./s), [Pantone](./p)

---

## Complementary Colours
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

Colours that sit directly opposite each other on the colour wheel (e.g. red and green, blue and orange). Used together they create maximum contrast and visual tension.

**Related:** [Analogous Colours](./a), [Triadic](./t), [Hue](./h)

---

## Crop Marks
**Category:** Print · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

Small lines printed at the corners of a document indicating where the paper should be cut. Also known as trim marks. Usually output outside the bleed area.

**Related:** [Bleed](./b), [Trim](./t), [Registration Marks](./r)

---

## Contrast
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The degree of visual difference between elements — in value (light vs dark), colour, size, weight, or texture. High contrast aids legibility and draws attention.

**Related:** [Hierarchy](./h), [Value](./v), [Legibility](./l)
