# H

## Hierarchy
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The arrangement of visual elements in order of importance. Hierarchy guides the viewer's eye through a design — typically achieved through differences in size, weight, colour, and position.

**Related:** [Contrast](./c), [Alignment](./a), [White Space](./w)

---

## Hue
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The pure colour itself — red, blue, yellow, etc. — before any tint or shade is applied. Hue is one of the three primary properties of colour alongside **saturation** and **value**.

**Related:** [Saturation](./s), [Value](./v), [Tint](./t), [Shade](./s)

---

## Hex Code
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A six-character hexadecimal notation for specifying colours in digital design (e.g. `#FF5733`). Each pair of characters represents the red, green, and blue channel values (00–FF).

**Related:** [RGB](./r), [CMYK](./c)
