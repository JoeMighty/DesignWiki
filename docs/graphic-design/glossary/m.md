# M

## Margin
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The blank space around the edges of a design or page that frames the content. Margins prevent text or images from appearing cramped against the edge and define the live area of a layout.

**Related:** [Grid](./g), [Gutter](./g), [White Space](./w)

---

## Mockup
**Category:** Digital · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A realistic visual representation of a finished design placed in context — e.g. a logo on a business card, or an app UI on a device screen. Mockups communicate intent to clients without building the final product.

**Related:** [Wireframe](./w), [Prototype](./p)

---

## Modular Scale
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

A typographic sizing system where each size level is multiplied by a consistent ratio (e.g. 1.25 or the golden ratio). Creates harmonious, proportional heading and body text hierarchies.

**Related:** [Hierarchy](./h), [Leading](./l), [Golden Ratio](./g)
