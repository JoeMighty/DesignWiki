# R

## Raster
**Category:** Digital · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A pixel-based image format (e.g. JPEG, PNG, TIFF). Raster images are resolution-dependent — enlarging them beyond their native size causes pixelation. Best used for photographs.

**Related:** [Vector](./v), [DPI](./d), [Resolution](./r)

---

## Resolution
**Category:** Digital · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The level of detail in a digital image, expressed as pixels per inch (PPI) for screen or dots per inch (DPI) for print. A high-resolution image has more detail and a larger file size.

**Related:** [DPI](./d), [Raster](./r), [PPI](./p)

---

## RGB
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Red, Green, Blue** — the additive colour model used for screens and digital displays. Mixing all three at full intensity produces white. RGB values are specified as numbers from 0–255.

**Related:** [CMYK](./c), [Hex Code](./h), [Colour Profile](./c)

---

## Rule of Thirds
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A compositional principle dividing an image into a 3×3 grid. Placing key elements along the gridlines or at their intersections creates more dynamic, visually engaging compositions than centring everything.

**Related:** [Golden Ratio](./g), [Balance](./b), [Hierarchy](./h)
