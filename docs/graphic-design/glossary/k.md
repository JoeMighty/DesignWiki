# K

## Kerning
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The manual adjustment of space between specific pairs of characters. Unlike tracking (which applies uniformly), kerning targets individual letter combinations — such as **AV**, **To**, or **We** — that would otherwise appear too close or too far apart.

**Example:** Compare "WAVE" with poor kerning vs. optically-balanced kerning.

**Related:** [Tracking](./t), [Leading](./l), [Ligature](./l)
