# D

## Descender
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The part of a lowercase letter that drops below the baseline — seen in **g**, **j**, **p**, **q**, and **y**. Descender depth varies between typefaces and affects perceived leading.

**Related:** [Ascender](./a), [Baseline](./b), [X-height](./x)

---

## DPI (Dots Per Inch)
**Category:** Print · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A measure of print resolution — the number of ink dots a printer can place per linear inch. For quality print output, a minimum of **300 dpi** at final print size is the industry standard.

**Related:** [PPI](./p), [Resolution](./r), [Raster](./r)

---

## Display Type
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

Type intended for large sizes — headlines, posters, or signage. Display typefaces are often more stylised and decorative, as they are not designed for extended reading at small sizes.

**Related:** [Body Text](./b), [Point Size](./p), [Typeface](./t)
