# P

## Pantone
**Category:** Colour Theory · **Difficulty:** Beginner

A standardised colour-matching system (PMS — Pantone Matching System) used globally in print to ensure consistent, reproducible colour across different presses and materials. Each Pantone colour has a unique number.

**Related:** [CMYK](./c), [Spot Colour](./s), [Colour Profile](./c)

---

## Point Size
**Category:** Typography · **Difficulty:** Beginner

The standard unit of typographic measurement. 1 point = 1/72 inch. Body text is typically 9–12 pt in print; display headings may be 24–72 pt or larger.

**Related:** [Leading](./l), [Em](./e), [Display Type](./d)

---

## Proximity
**Category:** Layout · **Difficulty:** Beginner

One of the core principles of design (Gestalt): related items should be placed close together, and unrelated items should be spaced apart. Proximity creates visual groupings without needing borders or rules.

**Related:** [Alignment](./a), [White Space](./w), [Hierarchy](./h)

---

## Prototype
**Category:** Digital · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

An interactive simulation of a product used for testing before development. Prototypes can range from clickable wireframes to high-fidelity interactive models.

**Related:** [Wireframe](./w), [Mockup](./m)
